Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter into the app.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the service, such as pages visited and actions taken.

We do not collect payment information. Spendly is a free tool and does not process any financial transactions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve the Spendly service:

  • To display your expense history and spending summaries within the app.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical precautions to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Your expense data is linked to your account and is not accessible to other users. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time through your profile settings or by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — to load typefaces used in the interface. Google may log your IP address when fonts are fetched.

We do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data broker services.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact information provided on our website.
